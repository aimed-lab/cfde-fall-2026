Step 1

Parking

  • Permit requiredCampus parking needs a permit seven days a week.
  • Closest: Lot P206Right beside the Faculty Club, but spaces are very limited.
  • Best bet: Scholars lotLarger, and the one to try if P206 is full. Lots P203–P205 on Scholars Drive South are also a short walk.
  • Leave extra timeNearby construction has reduced the number of spaces.

Step 2

Find your room

  • Covered Courtyard Check-in, breakfast, lunch and breaks
  • Atkinson Pavilion Main sessions · Tech Showcase A · Training and Communication & Outreach WGs
  • Seuss Library Tech Showcase B · Knowledge Graph WG · Steering Committee lunch
  • Classrooms 1–3 Tech Showcase C · Ontology WG
  • Classrooms 4–5 Tech Showcase D · Evaluation WG
